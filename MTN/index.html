Our purpose

Under the Ambition 2026 strategy, MTN aims to lead digital solutions for Africa’s progress, combining mobile, financial, and digital services to empower individuals, businesses, and governments. The strategy emphasizes leveraging connectivity infrastructure, mobile money, digital platforms, and technological innovations to improve access to services, drive financial inclusion, and support small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in their growth. MTN’s purpose is not limited to providing telecommunications; it extends to enabling societal and economic empowerment.

Our history

MTN, originally called M-Cell, was founded in South Africa in 1993 and has grown into Africa’s largest mobile telecommunications operator, with over 280 million subscribers worldwide.

Our strategic intent

MTN's strategic intent, under its Ambition 2025 plan, is to "Lead digital solutions for Africa's progress" by becoming Africa's largest and most valuable digital platform, focusing on building massive infrastructure, driving connectivity, offering fintech/digital services, and creating shared value. This involves transforming into a platform business with strong connectivity and digital offerings, leveraging partnerships, and improving efficiencies.
